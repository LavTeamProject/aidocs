Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Synthesia ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS application. Synthesia lets you generate AI videos from a text prompt and, optionally, your own reference photos.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in, we use Sign in with Apple and receive your Apple-provided identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication's REST API; we do not receive your Apple password.

Content you provide. The text prompts you write and any reference photos you upload (for example, face, location, or style references for the Montage feature) are sent to our servers and to the third-party AI providers we use to render your video, image, or script.

Purchase information. Subscriptions and coin purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We receive a signed purchase receipt (JWS) from Apple, which our servers verify to activate your subscription or credit your coin balance. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

Usage and device information. We may log basic technical information needed to operate the service, such as app version, generation task status, and error diagnostics.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate the videos, images, and scripts you request.
  • To authenticate you and maintain your account and coin balance.
  • To process and verify purchases and subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the app:

  • AI generation providers — your prompts and any uploaded reference photos are sent to the third-party AI models and rendering infrastructure we use to produce your video, image, or script.
  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and for processing in-app purchases and subscriptions (StoreKit).
  • Purchase analytics — successful purchase events are reported to our analytics/subscription-infrastructure provider so we can track subscription status; this does not include your payment details.
  • Legal requirements — if required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or lawful request.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data and generated content for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zyrexx@yandex.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children's Privacy

Synthesia is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted network connections and secure storage of authentication tokens on your device (in the iOS Keychain). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to this transfer, storage, and processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and in the app, and update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at zyrexx@yandex.com.
