Frequently Asked Questions

How does Synthesia generate videos?

You write a text prompt (and, for the Montage feature, optionally add reference photos of a face, location, or style). Synthesia sends your prompt and references to AI generation models, which produce your video, image, or shot-by-shot script.

What's the difference between Quick Clip and Montage?

Quick Clip generates a single clip from your prompt in one step. Montage is a two-step flow: an AI first builds a shot-by-shot script with dialogue and timing from your prompt and references, you review it and the price, and then confirm to render the final video.

How do coins work?

Generating a video or image costs coins. You get coins on sign-up, from an active subscription (credited automatically every billing period), or by purchasing a one-time coin pack. Coins are consumed on use and are non-refundable except where required by law.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Synthesia, and cancel or change your plan there. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" from inside the app.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are billed by Apple and subject to Apple's refund policies. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support — we're not able to issue refunds directly.

Whose photos can I upload as a reference?

Only upload photos you have the right to use — for example, yourself, or someone who has given you permission. Don't upload photos of other people without their consent, or content that violates our Terms of Use.

My generation failed or is stuck — what do I do?

Generation can occasionally fail due to a busy queue or an issue with the prompt. If a task fails, any coins spent on it are not charged. If a task looks stuck for more than a few minutes, contact support below with your account email and roughly when it happened.
